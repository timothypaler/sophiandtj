# Timothy & Sophia — In Pearl & Light

Wedding concept: 8 March 2027 · Aquila Crystal Palace, Tagaytay · #ASoph-isticatedTim-ing.

## Run or deploy

This is a buildless HTML/CSS/JavaScript website. The deployable site is in `dist/`; no Node installation or build command is required. The downloadable ZIP has the CONTENTS of `dist/` at its root, so `index.html` is immediately visible.

For GitHub Pages, extract the ZIP and upload its contents into your repository root. In repository Settings → Pages, select Deploy from a branch → main → /(root). For Cloudflare Pages, connect that repository, leave the build command empty and use the repository root as the output directory. Keep the assets, JavaScript and CSS beside index.html. Paths are relative, so GitHub project subpaths work.

Native Sites project metadata stays in `.openai/hosting.json`; it is not required in the GitHub export.

## Opening and design

The supplied 720 × 1280, 24fps opening video is losslessly remuxed for fast start, with its unused soundtrack omitted. It plays inline after the seal gesture and hands off to a pixel-identical final-frame hero image. The video is released afterward and restored for replay. Skip, media-error and reduced-motion fallbacks remain.

A continuous nacre/gold/floral background connects the sections, with moving light, pearl reflections, raised panel edges, native scroll-linked depth and fine-pointer tilt. All visible monograms use the crest extracted from the supplied video's final frame. The selected story card sits above its neighbours at full opacity with restrained glare, including chapter II.

The text-free nacre background was generated by editing the supplied final frame: remove names, date, venue, hashtag and monogram, preserving shell flowers, pearl striations, gilded edges and lighting. No generated people are included.

## Music

`assets/ikaw-ang-hiling.mp3` is the uploaded recording. It begins at 30% volume after touching the opening seal, loops, and has a visible play/pause control. Playback pauses when the tab is hidden. Browsers may require another tap on Play music. The video itself remains muted.

## Calendar and RSVP

The persistent Save the date button and the prominent FAQ button open Google, Outlook and Apple-compatible ICS options. The calendar reminder is all-day on 8 March 2027; guests confirm Add/Save in their calendar app. The countdown uses the sample 3 PM ceremony time in Asia/Manila.

RSVP is a clearly marked browser-local preview. It does not send or store guest responses. Put this wedding's real Google Form responder URL in `config.js` → `rsvpUrl` to replace the preview with a functioning form link. No unrelated event's form is used.

## Content and assets

The user authorized imagined details. The story, wedding party, attire, RSVP deadline and timings are illustrative and labelled as such. The venue illustration is decorative; the Maps link searches the supplied venue name without inventing an address.

The two framed stock photos are by Pixabay and Asad Photo Maldives via Pexels. Source links and the Pexels License are in `assets/photos/CREDITS.txt` and the page's photography credits. They do not depict Timothy and Sophia. Photography licensing does not cover the user-supplied recording.

Local fonts are Cormorant, Pinyon and DM Sans, with their OFL licenses in assets. No external JavaScript/CDN dependencies, database, particle pool or scroll hijacking are required. Reduced-motion and touch-friendly behavior are included.

## Mobile compatibility update
Story cards use a flat face swap with a restrained turn animation, avoiding nested 3D backface rendering and disabled-button text fading on iOS. No translucent card is painted above the active card. The clock sticks beside the full mobile timeline. Controls use smaller neutral pills with accessible tap targets. The poster stays visible until the first decoded video frame, preventing an empty frame at the seal gesture. The first photograph is the previously verified Foto Pettine hillside couple photo under the Unsplash License; music remains at 30%.
